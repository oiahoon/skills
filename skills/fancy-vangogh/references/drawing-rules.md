# Drawing rules grounded in the originals

These describe visible construction, not claims about the artist's software or actual working sequence. The anchor links lead to source articles crediting illustrator Alireza Karimi Moghaddam. See [artwork sources](artwork-sources.md) for the internal reference IDs.

## What has to transfer

| Layer | Visible rule | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Contour | Confident near-black curves define silhouettes, faces, hands and important objects. Most contours are fluid, not scratchy or artificially jittered. Interior marks are often lighter/thinner. | REF-0038, REF-0027, MMM-03-07. Environment mark fields can have few external outlines; do not black-outline every dash. |
| Character | Deliberately simplified facial features, expressive brows/eyes and hands, elastic posture and selective disproportion. Skin and clothing usually have large quiet fills with bounded shadow patches. | REF-0038, REF-0027, REF-0053. Long nose, beard and hat belong to depicted identities, not a mandatory style formula. MMM-02-01 is a marked-face counterexample, not a reason to texture every face. |
| Active environment | Smooth solid-color dashes, dots, arcs and longer streaks build crowns, water and ground over base colors. Many strokes have rounded ends and even interiors. Their alignment, overlap, gaps and local density form larger shapes. | MMM-01-07, REF-0044, REF-0053, MMM-01-04. Rounded/capsule strokes are not inherently wrong; random all-over stamping is. |
| Built environment | Drawn object edges and simple, slightly irregular perspective; walls use bounded broad color variations, wood long perspective-following strokes. | MMM-03-07, MMM-02-01. Clear perspective is legitimate; “crooked everything” is not a rule. |
| Color and value | Broad distinct color families establish shape; shadows often shift color in bounded patches. Local contrast makes the focal relationship legible. | REF-0038's blue/yellow, REF-0044's green/gold, MMM-03-07's dark blue/ochre. Do not force a muted warm palette on all scenes. When changing palette, transfer base/midtone/accent relationships instead of copying the literal reference hues; see the conditional palette routing in SKILL.md. |
| Composition | Pose, overlapping forms, major environment masses and stroke flow cooperate. A close character may dominate; elsewhere a tiny cartoon person sits inside a huge active landscape. | REF-0038 versus REF-0044/MMM-01-07. A broad blank area and a fixed character-size quota are both invalid universal rules. |
| Surface | The graphic clarity of the marks matters more than a generic claim of “brushiness.” Paint-like references may still have smooth hard-edged marks rather than bristle grain or thick impasto. | All anchors. Some original regions have tonal variation; “flat” does not mean sterile vector geometry or banning every subtle color transition. |

In a complex generated plaza, repeated pebble-shaped strokes across nearly the entire ground and confetti-like foliage can imitate the reference's local marks while losing its larger masses and varied density. First locate the main trunks, crown groups, paths and quiet gaps; then vary stroke direction, spacing and size within those forms. Likewise, a recognizable face can be too photo-derived when the eyelids, cheeks and hair are rendered with portrait detail instead of a few designed marks. Judge both at full-image and face scale.

REF-0038 and REF-0027 show how exposed white shapes, partially covering lids and displaced pupils cooperate with unequal brows to express gaze. MMM-02-03 has a more open-eyed expression, while REF-0012 also supports dots and closed-lid curves. These are alternatives; simplification does not require removing visible eye-white from a focal person. Preserve individual eye opening, gaze and eyewear occlusion. Contours remain confident flowing curves with selective weight and unequal turns, not a uniform border or a compulsory rough stroke. See [feature and contour construction](character-environment.md).

For pointed grasses and stems, distinguish plant geometry from the general mark field: MMM-05-01 mixes fine tapering wheat stems with shorter groups, while MMM-02-03 uses lance-shaped iris leaf planes. REF-0044 also uses rounded/bent field marks. Preserve this mixture and distance scaling; do not cover an entire hill with identical thick pointed blades.

For interior depth, MMM-03-07 uses a large cropped foreground furniture mass, overlapping middle figures and a quieter rear wall, with diagonal floor bands linking them. Transfer those compositional operations rather than its bed/easel layout. Lens distortion alone cannot supply this hierarchy.

Use another relevant original when available. These thirteen core anchors are a working set, not a claim to cover the entire artist's range. Six additional full originals and conditional sky guidance are catalogued in [sky and light-source construction](sky-and-light.md). Do not pick water merely because a generated water test looked good when the new scene is dry.
