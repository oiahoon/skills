# Calibration and evidence

The drawing rules were developed from works credited to Alireza Karimi Moghaddam. See [artwork sources](artwork-sources.md) for the anchor index. The observations describe visible shapes and marks.

- **People.** Keep individual head, hair, facial and gesture relationships. Ordinary figures often use connected colors and controlled contours beside denser scenery. Some original painted-face regions use markedly different detail.
- **Materials.** Rich marks should build larger forms. Check near and far crowns separately, distinguish flat calm water from rolling bands, and use edge contrast or ink according to each material. Daisy color edges can coexist with outlined hands and folded iris petals.
- **Content.** Inspect eye visibility, counts, supports and hand contacts separately from drawing quality. For a rope or cable, trace both ends and every visible segment. In a wide-angle scene, check the entire requested object as well as the projection.
- **Photo redraws.** A source-derived drawing can preserve useful contacts while still looking photographic or losing character detail. Promote a guide only after inspecting its actual feature construction, occlusions and relevant contacts.

Development probes showed local gains for flower edges, shelter boundaries, connector paths, cloud coverage and photo-derived character drawing. Generic faces, copied motifs, repetitive marks and missing details still occurred. The public edition uses written guidance and source links. Reference-driven matching depends on the actual pictures available to the host.
